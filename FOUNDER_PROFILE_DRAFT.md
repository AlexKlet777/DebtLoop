## Founder statement draft

“I created DebtLoop around a simple observation: many businesses can be short of cash at the same time even when money is effectively trapped inside circular chains of receivables and payables. DebtLoop is designed to identify those chains and turn them into transparent, auditable netting proposals. My role is to validate the commercial problem, build the right partnerships, and turn the working proof of concept into a scalable B2B product for Qatar and the wider GCC market.”
